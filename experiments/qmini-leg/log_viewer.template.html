Body tilt comes from the IMU gravity vector, and the legs from the encoder angles. Foot world pitch combines the two (IMU tilt plus encoder chain) in the foot frame. A foot flat on the floor reads 0°. Anything else means the heel or toe lifted, or there is play the encoders can't see (gear backlash, flex, sole), or the IMU or joint zero is off.
